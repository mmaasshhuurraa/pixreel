# Demand & SEO landscape for "photos to video" / slideshow tools (global + RU)

Access date for all sources: 2026-11-05. Research environment limits (important for the report writer):
- trends.google.com, ahrefs.com and clideo.com were **blocked** by the session's network proxy (connection refused / DNS fail). No direct Google Trends, Ahrefs, Semrush keyword tool or Yandex Wordstat readings could be taken.
- Only a US-based web search tool was available; it is **not** Google or Yandex SERP itself. "Who ranks" lists below are what that search engine returned on 2026-11-05 and are a proxy for, not a copy of, Google/Yandex top-10.
- No public article found quoting Wordstat or Keyword Planner numbers for any of the target phrases. **No monthly-volume figures are reported here; none were invented.**

## 1. Keyword clusters and approximate monthly search volumes (EN + RU)

### Takeaway
Exact volumes could not be obtained from public sources in this session; the market is clearly searched in several distinct clusters (slideshow maker / photo-to-video maker / format converters "jpg to mp4" / AI "image to video"), and vendors build a separate landing page per cluster. Volumes must be pulled manually from Keyword Planner / Wordstat before prioritising.

### Cited Findings
- Multiple searches for published volumes of "slideshow maker", "photo to video", "jpg to mp4", "gif to mp4", "images to video" returned only tool-methodology pages, no numbers — e.g. [Semrush free volume checker](https://www.semrush.com/free-tools/keyword-search-volume-checker/), [Keywords Everywhere](https://keywordseverywhere.com/google-search-volume.html), [searchvolume.io](https://searchvolume.io/).
- Only number found for a slideshow phrase: MerchantWords (Amazon search, not Google) shows ~300 searches for "movavi slideshow maker 2025" and <100 for "movavi slideshow maker" — [MerchantWords](https://www.merchantwords.com/search/de/sideshow). Irrelevant to web search, noted for completeness.
- Keyword Planner/Semrush volumes are 12-month averages and rounded; treat as estimates — [Keywords Everywhere](https://keywordseverywhere.com/blog/monthly-search-volume/), [Semrush KD blog](https://www.semrush.com/blog/keyword-difficulty/).
- Wordstat: report covers last 28 days; base frequency includes all word forms/orders and is inflated ("реальный спрос на конкретную фразу в разы меньше"); exact phrase check uses `"!фото !в !видео"` — [Яндекс Практикум](https://practicum.yandex.ru/blog/statistika-zaprosov-yandex-wordstat/), [Timeweb](https://timeweb.com/ru/community/articles/chastotnost-ne-ravna-sprosu-kak-razbirat-wordstat-pered-sozdaniem-stranicy), [elama](https://elama.ru/blog/kak-polzovatsya-yandeks-wordstat-instrukciya-dlya-nachinayuschih/).
- Pictory distinguishes "slideshow maker" (many photos → video) from "image to video" (AI-animating a single picture) — [Pictory](https://pictory.ai/slideshow-maker). Dreamina/CapCut positions its "JPG to MP4" page as AI animation of a single photo — [Dreamina](https://dreamina.capcut.com/resource/jpg-to-mp4-converter).
- Vendors run separate pages per phrasing: VEED has both "/create/photo-video-maker" and "/create/slideshow-video-maker" — [VEED photo video maker](https://www.veed.io/create/photo-video-maker), [VEED slideshow](https://www.veed.io/create/slideshow-video-maker); FlexClip "/features/photo-to-video" — [FlexClip](https://flexclip.com/features/photo-to-video); Movavi "/tools/video-from-photos/" — [Movavi](https://www.movavi.com/tools/video-from-photos/); Adobe Express "/express/create/video/photo" — [Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/express/create/video/photo).
- RU vendors also use per-intent pages: SUPA "/features/slideshow-with-music" — [SUPA](https://supa.ru/features/slideshow-with-music); GenPic "/slideshow-iz-foto" — [GenPic](https://genpic.ai/slideshow-iz-foto); Kwork landing "слайд-шоу с музыкой" (service-ordering intent, from 500 ₽) — [Kwork](https://kwork.ru/land/slayd-shou-s-muzykoy).
- RU SEO guides recommend Key Collector / Wordstat Assistant / Bukvarix to collect frequency — [pawetta](https://pawetta.com/blog/kak-sobrat-semanticheskoe-yadro/).

### Inferences
- Observed clusters (from competitor URL structure, not volume data): (a) "slideshow maker [with music / free / no watermark / online]"; (b) "photo(s) to video maker", "make video from pictures"; (c) format converters "jpg/png/gif/heic/webp to mp4"; (d) AI "image to video" — a different intent (generative animation) that is crowding the SERP; (e) RU: «слайд-шоу из фото (с музыкой)», «видео из фото онлайн», «программа для слайд-шоу» (desktop intent dominated by ФотоШОУ/Movavi), plus service intent («заказать слайд-шоу»).
- Cluster (d) is likely the fastest-growing but PixReel does not serve it; avoid targeting "image to video" head term without qualifying ("photos to video", "slideshow").

### Gaps
- All monthly volumes (EN and RU) — need manual Keyword Planner / Semrush / Wordstat pull. Suggested list for that pull: the 11 EN and 6 RU phrases from the brief + "png to mp4", "webp to mp4", "heic to mp4", "images to avi", "gif to mp4", "timelapse from photos", «фото в видео онлайн», «видео из фото с музыкой».

## 2. Long-tail / converter-style queries

### Takeaway
Format-pair converter pages ("JPG to MP4", "JPEG to M4V", "Image to MP4") are a well-established SEO tactic run at scale by converter sites (XConvert, AnyConv, Movavi, Wondershare, Clideo, CapCut/Dreamina); PixReel could capture them with lightweight programmatic landing pages, differentiating on "no upload".

### Cited Findings
- XConvert has separate pages for jpg→mp4, jpg→mpg, jpg→mpeg, jpeg→m4v, image→mp4 (programmatic format pairs) — [XConvert jpg-to-mp4](https://www.xconvert.com/convert-jpg-to-mp4), [jpeg-to-m4v](https://www.xconvert.com/convert-jpeg-to-m4v), [image-to-mp4](https://www.xconvert.com/convert-image-to-mp4).
- Movavi has separate "convert-jpeg-to-mp4" and "convert-jpg-to-mp4" pages (spelling variants targeted individually) — [Movavi JPEG](https://www.movavi.com/image-converter/convert-jpeg-to-mp4/), [Movavi JPG](https://www.movavi.com/image-converter/convert-jpg-to-mp4/).
- Other ranking converter pages: [AnyConv](https://anyconv.com/jpeg-to-mp4-converter) (states files deleted after one hour — i.e. server upload), [Wondershare](https://videoconverter.wondershare.com/convert-mp4/jpg-to-mp4.html), [Clideo how-to](https://clideo.com/amp/how-to-convert-jpg-to-mp4), [VideoProc](https://www.videoproc.com/video-editor/convert-jpg-to-mp4.htm), [convertintomp4](https://convertintomp4.com/zh/jpg-to-mp4) (localized variants).
- Browser-only (WASM/FFmpeg) competitors already market "nothing is uploaded": [SvenStudios reel creator](https://svenstudios.com/free-simple-photo-slideshow-reel-creator), [Favtoo video slideshow maker](https://favtoo.pages.dev/video-slideshow-maker) (FFmpeg compiled to WebAssembly).

### Inferences
- Converter-intent users want one action, no editor; a PixReel landing per pair (e.g. /jpg-to-mp4, /heic-to-mp4, /png-to-mp4, /gif-to-mp4, /webp-to-mp4, RU «jpg в mp4») with the dropzone above the fold and "files never leave your device" vs AnyConv-style "deleted after 1 hour" is a concrete differentiator.
- HEIC→MP4 is plausibly underserved (no dedicated pages surfaced in the searches), but unverified.

### Gaps
- Volumes for each format pair; no data found. "webp to gif", "images to avi", "heic to mp4" specific SERPs not checked (tool-call budget).

## 4. Seasonality and trend direction 2022–2026

### Takeaway
No direct Trends data could be read. Qualitative evidence suggests event-driven demand (weddings, holidays, graduation) and a strong 2024–2026 shift of attention toward AI "image to video", but these are not measured here.

### Cited Findings
- Canva's "Wedding Slideshow" template search returns 14,000+ results (supply signal for wedding use case) — [onreplay.ai](https://onreplay.ai/blog/best-video-creation-tools-photos/).
- Movavi RU positions its slideshow maker for quick holiday videos — [Movavi RU](https://www.movavi.com/ru/adv/slideshow-maker.html).
- Claims of a pivot toward AI video generators in search data are qualitative, low-authority — [vocal.media](https://vocal.media/futurism/the-next-frontier-of-generative-ai-why-image-to-video-tools-are-surging-right-now); Google Year in Search 2025 is AI-heavy (Gemini top search) without image-to-video breakdown — [Android Central](https://www.androidcentral.com/apps-software/googles-2025-search-data-reveals-a-world-gripped-by-gemini-and-chaos).
- FlexClip Semrush visits declined Oct→Dec 2025 (4.22M→3.66M) — [Semrush](https://cw.semrush.com/website/flexclip.com/overview) (single short window; may be seasonal or noise).

### Inferences
- Expected (unverified) peaks: May–June (graduation, Mother's Day, «последний звонок»/выпускной in RU), Dec–Jan (New Year recap videos — strong in RU), wedding season Jun–Sep, plus 8 Марта / 23 февраля / birthdays in RU. Validate with Trends/Wordstat history before planning content.
