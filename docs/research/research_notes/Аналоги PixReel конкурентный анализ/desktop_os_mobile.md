# Desktop software, OS built-ins and mobile apps that compete with PixReel

All sources were accessed on 2026-11-05. Method note: direct fetches of vendor sites failed because clipchamp.com and fotoshow-pro.ru were outside the session's allowed network domains. Most facts below therefore come from search-result content: vendor pages where the search surfaced them, otherwise third-party reviews and stores. Where sources conflict, both are given. Anything sourced only to a third-party review or catalogue should be treated as **unverified** until someone checks the vendor page.

## Q1. What exists, on which platforms, and how do they work (local or cloud)?

### Takeaway
The market splits four ways:
- **Free OS built-ins:** Clipchamp, iMovie, Google Photos and Samsung Gallery. Some are account- or cloud-tied.
- **Cheap one-time-licence Windows slideshow tools:** ФотоШОУ PRO/SmartSHOW 3D, Movavi/«Мовавика», Icecream, Bolide, PhotoStage, Ashampoo.
- **Free open-source NLEs:** Shotcut, OpenShot, Kdenlive and PhotoFilmStrip.
- **Freemium mobile editors:** CapCut, InShot, VN, KineMaster, Quik and others.

None of them is zero-install, no-signup and browser-local the way PixReel is. Clipchamp is the closest in that it runs in the browser, but it needs a Microsoft account.

### Cited Findings
**OS built-ins**
- **Microsoft Clipchamp (Windows / web)**
  - Clipchamp replaced the Video Editor in the Windows Photos app.
  - The legacy storyboard editor can still be reached through "Photos Legacy", which is available from the Microsoft Store and can run in parallel with the new Photos app — [gHacks](https://www.ghacks.net/2024/03/06/how-to-restore-the-photos-legacy-app-on-windows/); [OnMSFT](https://onmsft.com/news/windows-10-photos-app-revamp-microsoft-swaps-native-video-editor-for-clipchamp/); [Windows Latest](https://www.windowslatest.com/2023/09/11/microsoft-is-replacing-windows-10s-video-editor-with-web-based-clipchamp/)
  - The date of the removal is inconsistent across Wikipedia articles (2022 vs. a "2026 Windows 11 update") — [Wikipedia: Photos (Windows)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photos_(Windows)); [Wikipedia: Windows Movie Maker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windows_Movie_Maker)
  - Some users preferred the old editor because it was simpler and produced much smaller files than Clipchamp — [itechguides](https://www.itechguides.com/how-to-merge-videos-in-windows-11-10-photos-legacy-and-clipchamp/)
  - Microsoft ended iOS support for Clipchamp in June 2026 without a replacement — [Wikipedia: Microsoft Clipchamp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Clipchamp)
  - A Microsoft account login is required — [leantech.ai (RU)](https://leantech.ai/ai/clipchamp)
- **Apple iMovie (Mac / iPhone / iPad)**
  - Works with 4K/60fps "as long as one of the clips in your project was originally recorded in one of those formats" — [Apple Support 102276](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102276)
  - On Mac, 4K is chosen under Share → File → Resolution. The 4K option is greyed out when the project has no 4K source clips — [Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102276); [ourstoria (3rd party)](https://ourstoria.app/blog/how-to-export-imovie-to-mp4)
- **Google Photos Memories / highlight videos**
  - A Memory consists of a highlight video, which is what gets shared, plus an album of the source media. Users edit through "Edit" in the highlight editor — [Google Photos Help](https://support.google.com/photos/answer/9454489?hl=en-AU&co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid)
  - On the web, "highlight videos" replace the old "movies", via Create → "Create your own video" — [Google Photos Help: Make creations](https://support.google.com/photos/answer/6128862?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop); [How-To Geek](https://www.howtogeek.com/dont-share-that-google-photos-memory-yet-make-these-edits-first/)
  - Google Photos now makes automatic highlight videos — [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/computing/software/google-photos-can-now-make-automatic-highlights-videos-of-your-life-heres-how)
  - Editing has historically been minimal: mute/unmute and removing images. Upgrades, such as image filters in the highlight editor, have been reported — [Android Police](https://www.androidpolice.com/google-photos-memories-edit-upgrade/)

### Inferences
- Clipchamp being browser-based makes it PixReel's most direct "built-in" rival on Windows. Its account requirement and its server-side features (stock, AI) remain points where PixReel can differentiate (no signup, local-only).
- Apple's 4K rule (a 4K source clip is required) means photo-only iMovie slideshows may be capped below 4K. That is an angle for PixReel's "4K from photos" claim, but it should be verified hands-on.

### Gaps
- Install sizes and system requirements were not retrieved for any product, because vendor sites could not be fetched.
- Samsung Gallery Stories/Video Editor: no search results were found.
- Apple Photos Memories export options: not found.
- For Windows Photos, it is unclear whether a "Photos Legacy" video editor is still downloadable in late 2026.
- Privacy: whether Clipchamp renders in the browser or in the cloud, and the processing location for Google Photos and CapCut AI, were not confirmed from primary sources.

## Q2. Input and output formats, max resolution

### Takeaway
Desktop RU-market tools (ФотоШОУ, Movavi) and Bolide offer the legacy AVI/WMV outputs that PixReel also has. Mobile apps and OS built-ins output MP4/MOV. No source confirmed HEIC, RAW, PSD or PDF input for any slideshow tool, so PixReel's planned server-side formats could be a differentiator, though this is unverified.

### Cited Findings
- **ФотоШОУ PRO:** "30+ video formats" plus DVD burning — [FreeSoft](https://freesoft.ru/windows/fotoshou_pro)
  - The formats listed include AVI, MP4, 3GP, MKV and DivX, but that list comes from a warez/repack page — [basarunet (low quality)](https://basarunet.org/soft/graf/79890-fotoshou-pro-142-repack-repack-amp-portable-by-tryroom.html)
  - Uncompressed AVI or image sequence (JPEG/PNG) export — [DTF](https://dtf.ru/software/4548535-fotoshou-pro-skachat-programmu-dlya-sozdaniya-slid-shou)
- **SmartSHOW 3D Deluxe:** "export options to 30+ video formats" — [subscribed.fyi](https://subscribed.fyi/smartshow-3d/pricing/)
- **Movavi Slideshow Maker:** WMV, MOV, MP4, AVI — [pdfchef](https://www.pdfchef.com/best-slideshow-maker/)
  - "180+ media formats", including MKV, FLV and WebM, and 720p/1080p; one source mentions 4K — [Tipard](https://www.tipard.com/video/movavi-slideshow-maker-review.html); [dealarious](https://www.dealarious.com/blog/movavi-slideshow-maker-review/)
- **Icecream Slideshow Maker:**
  - Free export is reported as **WebM only** and below 1080p; Pro adds 2K/4K — [MajorGeeks](https://www.majorgeeks.com/files/details/icecream_slideshow_maker.html); [Capterra UK](https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/183297/icecream-slideshow-maker)
  - The vendor's page should be checked, because sources disagree on the free tier — [mspoweruser](https://mspoweruser.com/icecream-slideshow-maker-review/)
- **Bolide Slideshow Creator:** AVI, MKV, FLV, MP4 and WMV, according to the vendor site — [vendor](https://slideshow-creator.com/photo-slideshow-software.html)
- **PhotoFilmStrip:** up to 1920×1080 — [SourceForge](https://sourceforge.net/projects/photostoryx/)
- **iMovie:** 4K/60 only if a source clip is 4K/60 — [Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102276)
- **Clipchamp:** free plan exports up to 1080p; 4K requires Premium (per the official pricing page, as reported in search) — [Clipchamp pricing](https://clipchamp.com/en/pricing/); [aiarty review](https://www.aiarty.com/edit-video/clipchamp-review.htm)
- **VN:** free export up to 4K/60fps — [pixflow](https://pixflow.net/blog/best-mobile-video-editing-apps/); [RexJafor](https://rexjafor.com/blog/post/vn-video-editor-review)
- **Filmora Basic:** includes 4K export with no watermark — [bityclips](https://bityclips.com/tool/wondershare-filmora/pricing)

### Inferences
- GIF output and the AVI/WMV/MKV breadth together are uncommon among mobile and OS tools. PixReel's output breadth matches the older Windows slideshow programs rather than modern editors.

### Gaps
- No primary-source data was found on HEIC, RAW, PSD or PDF input, or on animated-GIF import, for any product. Hands-on testing or vendor help pages are needed.
- Clipchamp's output container options (MP4 only? GIF?) were not confirmed.

## Q3. Free-version limits, effects, transitions, music and AI

### Takeaway
Typical paywalls are watermarks (KineMaster, InShot, the ФотоШОУ/Movavi trials), time-limited trials (7 days for Movavi, 10 days for ФотоШОУ), photo-count caps (Icecream: 10 photos) and resolution caps (Clipchamp: 1080p free). Truly free, no-watermark options are Clipchamp up to 1080p, VN, Bolide, the open-source tools and the OS apps.

### Cited Findings
- **Clipchamp:**
  - Free plan exports up to 1080p with no watermark — [Clipchamp pricing (via search)](https://clipchamp.com/en/pricing/)
  - One review claims a "Made with Clipchamp" watermark, which conflicts with the official page — [bityclips](https://bityclips.com/blog/clipchamp-review-2026)
  - Projects that use premium stock cannot be exported on the free plan — [fluxnote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/clipchamp-free-plan-limitations)
- **ФотоШОУ PRO:**
  - 10-day trial with a watermark on all exports — [SoftHome](https://www.softhome.ru/program/fotoshou-pro)
  - Transition counts vary by source: "100+" [basarunet](https://basarunet.org/soft/graf/79890-fotoshou-pro-142-repack-repack-amp-portable-by-tryroom.html), "150+ including 3D" [FreeSoft](https://freesoft.ru/windows/fotoshou_pro), "~200" for v26.0 [softload](https://softload.su/graf/430-fotoshou-pro.html)
  - Transitions can be assigned automatically — [same](https://freesoft.ru/windows/fotoshou_pro)
- **SmartSHOW 3D Deluxe:** "200+ transitions and animation effects" vs. "400+" — [subscribed.fyi](https://subscribed.fyi/smartshow-3d/pricing/); [Filmora 3D slideshow roundup](https://filmora.wondershare.com/video-editing/3d-slideshow.html)
- **Movavi Slideshow Maker:** 7-day trial, watermark, and audio saved at only half length — [Tipard](https://www.tipard.com/video/movavi-slideshow-maker-review.html); [anymp4](https://www.anymp4.com/video-editing/movavi-slideshow-maker.html)
- **Icecream:**
  - 10 photos per slideshow on the free tier; personal use only — [Tipard](https://www.tipard.com/video/icecream-slideshow-maker-review.html); [VideoHelp](https://www.videohelp.com/software/Icecream-Slideshow-Maker)
  - Watermark: the vendor and wnsoft say none, FileHorse says yes — [vendor](https://icecreamapps.com/Slideshow-Maker/); [wnsoft](https://www.wnsoft.com/en/blog/best-photo-slideshow-software/); [FileHorse](https://www.filehorse.com/download-icecream-slideshow-maker/)
- **Bolide:** no watermark, no trial limit (vendor claim) — [vendor FAQ](https://slideshow-creator.com/faq.html)
- **PhotoStage:** one source says the free version is limited to one month; another says it is free for non-commercial use — [softwaretestinghelp](https://www.softwaretestinghelp.com/slideshow-maker-software/); [NCH](https://www.nchsoftware.com/software/best/best-slideshow-maker.html)
- **InShot:** the free tier has a watermark and ads, and Pro removes both — [frameos](https://frameos.studio/blog/best-inshot-alternatives); [unstore](https://unstore.io/discover/best-inshot-alternatives/)
- **KineMaster:** watermark on the free tier, none on Premium — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/233236/KineMaster/reviews/?page=2); [eesel](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/capcut-alternatives)
- **VN:** no watermark and no ads on the free tier — [pixflow](https://pixflow.net/blog/best-mobile-video-editing-apps/)
- **GoPro Quik:** free to download, with the subscription unlocking editing features. The subscription includes unlimited cloud backup — [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/pro/software-services/gopro-quik-review); [GoPro](https://gopro.com/en/us/news/quik-unlimited-mural-backup)
- **CapCut in Russia:**
  - Users report that many features are blocked "for your country" — [RuStore reviews](https://www.rustore.ru/catalog/app/com.lemon.lvoverseas/reviews)

### Inferences
- PixReel's 13 transitions is far below the desktop RU leaders (ФотоШОУ/SmartSHOW at 100–400+ effects), but PixReel competes on zero friction, not effect count.
- Multi-track music with per-track volume and crossfade is a feature the cheap slideshow tools are rarely documented to have. This needs hands-on verification.

### Gaps
- Ken Burns, template counts, text tools, AI features (auto-cut, beat sync) and music-track handling (multiple tracks, per-track volume, crossfade, beat sync) were not found in reliable sources for most products.
- Data on VivaVideo, Splice and YouCut was not found; the search returned nothing for them.
